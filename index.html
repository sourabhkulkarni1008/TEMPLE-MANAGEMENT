Shree Siddhivinayak Temple Information & Guidelines

Daily schedules, dress code, Annadanam, and facilities

Daily Pooja & Aarti Timings

  • 05:00 AM: Suprabhata Seva & Temple Gate Opening
  • 06:00 AM - 12:30 PM: Morning Darshan Queue Slots
  • 12:30 PM - 03:30 PM: Madhyahna Pooja & Annadanam Break
  • 04:00 PM - 07:00 PM: Evening General Darshan
  • 07:00 PM: Maha Deeparadhana / Grand Evening Aarti
  • 08:00 PM - 10:30 PM: Night Darshan & Temple Closure

Devotee Guidelines & Etiquette

  • Traditional Dress Code: Men are requested to wear Dhoti/Kurta; Women in Saree or Salwar Kameez.
  • Electronic Items: Mobile phones must be switched to silent mode. Photography inside the Garbhagriha is strictly prohibited.
  • Footwear Storage: Free secured footwear deposit stands are operational adjacent to Gate 1 and Gate 2.
  • Holy Prasadam: Certified Laddus and Panchamritam counters operate at the exit corridor.
